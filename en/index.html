CoupleNest · 1.0

A little home for everyday memories

A cozy 2D home with two characters, a cat, notes and photo memories — kept on your iPhone.

iPhone · iOS 17 or later · Free · No in-app purchases

Updated: 2026-09-17

Make a little space for the two of you

Change the outfits of two characters in a warm pixel-style room and tap the cat for a small interaction. Keep the moments you want to remember close by.

Remember everyday things

Set your relationship start date, view the days together, write notes, tick off tasks, and save memories with text and an optional selected photo. The interface supports English, Simplified Chinese, Greek and Albanian.

One iPhone, local data

Version 1.0 works on one device. It has no partner account linking, messaging service or cross-device synchronization. Notes are not sent to another person. Core features work without an internet connection; opening support or privacy pages uses your browser.

Simple to use

No sign-in, advertising, subscription or in-app purchase is required. Read the help page for photo import, date counting and data storage details.
