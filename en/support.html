Couple Home · 1.1

Help & support

A cozy 2D home with two characters, a cat, notes and photo memories — kept on your iPhone.

iPhone · iOS 17 or later · Free · No in-app purchases

Updated: 2026-10-27

Using your home

Open Home to see the room, two characters and cat. Open the wardrobe to change either character's outfit. Tap the cat to interact. In Settings, choose a language or follow the iPhone's language.

Save a travel picture

From Home, open Travel together, choose a place and select a couple pose. Tap Save a memory, review the title, add a note if you want, and tap Save. The app stores an 800 × 1200 JPEG in Memories on this iPhone. Nothing is posted or sent to your partner. The picture uses the included artwork; it does not need access to your photo library or location.

Change a look and browse artwork

In the wardrobe, choose Original or Short-hair look separately for each character. The short-hair option includes its own fixed outfit. Choose Original again to use the original outfit choices. From Home, open Art collection to view the scenes, poses, postcards and studies. The six poses are still illustrations, and the short-hair couple variant is available for holding hands. Study images are not selectable skin tones or separate clothing layers.

Dates, notes and tasks

Set a past or current relationship start date in Settings. The starting date counts as day 0, and the counter uses calendar dates in the Pacific/Auckland time zone. Notes and tasks can be edited or deleted; tasks can be marked complete. These are local lists, without scheduled notification alerts or delivery to another person.

Photo memories

Create a memory with a title, text and an optional image chosen through Apple's photo picker. The app makes a JPEG display copy, up to 1280 pixels on its longest side; it does not modify the original in Photos. The selected input file must be at most 12 MiB. If an image is too large or cannot be opened, choose another image or save the memory without a photo.

Keep your existing data

Use a normal App Store update instead of uninstalling when you want to keep your existing data. Uninstalling removes the app's local data. There is no in-app export, partner synchronization or developer recovery service. Keep original photos and your own copies of important text. Apple device backup or restore may include app data depending on your settings; recovery is not guaranteed by Couple Home.

If saving or display fails

First return to Home or reopen the app. If a save or loading error continues, check available iPhone storage and contact support. Avoid uninstalling while investigating a data problem. The app keeps an earlier local state to reduce accidental overwrite, but this is not an independent backup.
