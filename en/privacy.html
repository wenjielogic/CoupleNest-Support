CoupleNest · 1.0

Privacy

A cozy 2D home with two characters, a cat, notes and photo memories — kept on your iPhone.

iPhone · iOS 17 or later · Free · No in-app purchases

Updated: 2026-09-17

Scope and operator

This policy describes CoupleNest 1.0 and its support website, provided by Wenjie Lu. The app works without creating an account. Its core features do not send your notes or photos to a developer-operated server.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your chosen start date, interface language, outfit choices, notes and author labels, task text and completion status, memory titles and text, timestamps, local record identifiers and selected photo copies. This information is used to display your home and keep your entries between launches. There is no partner binding, message delivery or app-managed cloud synchronization.

Photo selection

Apple's system photo picker gives the app the image you choose; full photo-library access is not required. The app creates a local JPEG display copy with a longest side of up to 1280 pixels. The original in Photos is not edited or deleted. Apple may retrieve a selected image from iCloud according to your device settings. The developer does not receive the image.

Servers, advertising and analytics

The app has no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics SDK, third-party crash-reporting SDK, in-app purchase or developer account server. We do not sell your app data. Apple may process App Store activity or diagnostics under Apple's own policies and your settings; the app does not add an analytics service.

Retention and deletion

Your entries remain in the app's local storage until you delete them or remove the app. An earlier valid local state is also retained for recovery protection. After deleting an entry, that earlier state or its referenced photo may remain until later successful saves and cleanup replace it. This copy is stored with the app on the same device, not in a separate backup service. Uninstalling removes the app's local data. The app does not promise secure erasure of storage or deletion of copies held in device backups.

Device backups and security

Depending on your Apple backup settings, app data may be included in an iCloud or computer device backup. CoupleNest does not control those backups or guarantee restoration, and it does not provide an export feature. Protect your iPhone with a device passcode and keep your own copies of important content. The app does not offer a separate password vault or promise end-to-end encryption.

Website and support email

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no analytics scripts, advertising cookies or contact form added by us. GitHub may process ordinary web request information, such as IP address, under its privacy statement. If you email support, your sender address, message and attachments are handled through email providers to answer your request. Keep messages limited to what is necessary. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and legitimate recordkeeping; provider backups may have their own retention.

Your choices and contact

Photo import is optional. You can choose no photo, edit or delete entries in the app, and control Apple device backups in your device settings. We cannot remotely access or delete the local contents of your iPhone. Contact ljj781325@gmail.com to ask about this policy or to request access, correction or deletion of support correspondence. Changes to data practices will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
